# Planning interview skill

Charter-level discovery — not SRS depth. Per-area topics come from the active area guidance in the prompt.

## Style
- Propose-then-confirm for thin areas (risks, scope, stakeholders) when no facts exist yet.
- One sharp question per turn with 3–6 options plus Other.
- Stop when Planning essentials are clear; do not chase stack, budget, or timeline detail (out of v1).
- Do not invent metrics, vendors, or compliance regimes the user did not state.
