# Requirements interview skill

Deepen product behavior without re-asking accepted Charter ground (D27). Per-area topics come from the active area guidance in the prompt.

## Style
- Confirm inherited product boundary; do not re-litigate Charter scope.
- One sharp question per turn with 3–6 options plus Other.
- When facts already exist, confirm — but only close on explicit confirmation or rubric met.
- If the user picks one chip from a multi-part question, treat as partial and ask what else applies.
- Enough for Requirements depth — do not pad with speculative NFR numbers.
